Keywords Everywhere is a Chrome, Firefox, and Edge extension that overlays search volume, CPC, competition, and related keyword ideas on Google and 15+ other sites while you browse. For a solo mobile developer it is a landing-page and content-research tool, not an App Store ASO tool.

It earns a place if you are writing launch-page copy or blog posts and you want Google demand data without standing up a full SEO suite. If the next job is the App Store listing, skip it and use an ASO tool instead. For the longer decision, see [Keywords Everywhere for indie app developers](/guides/keywords-everywhere-indie-mobile-developers).

Best for: landing-page and content research in Google. Not good for: App Store listing research, rank tracking, or Safari and iPhone use.

## Pricing
Keywords Everywhere is freemium. The extension installs at no cost. Search volume, CPC, competition, and trend data run on credits sold as annual plans.

:::comparison Keywords Everywhere plans (checked Sep 21, 2026)
| Plan | Price | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | Keyword ideas, traffic and Moz metrics, SEO reports, social insights, prompt templates. No search volume. |
| Bronze | $84 / year | 100,000 credits, 1 seat. Full extension and API. Usual plan for a solo builder. |
| Silver | $168 / year | 400,000 credits, 3 seats. Adds SEO Minion and premium ChatGPT prompts. |
| Gold | $480 / year | 2,000,000 credits, 10 seats. Adds Keyword Keg and RapidLevelUp courses. |
| Platinum | $1,440 / year | 8,000,000 credits, 20 seats. Priority email support. |
:::

There are no monthly plans. Credits expire 12 months after purchase. One credit buys volume, CPC, competition, and the 12-month trend for one keyword. A Google search that shows 8 Related, 20 People Also Search For, and 25 Long-tail keywords uses 54 credits. Pricing last checked Sep 21, 2026; confirm current numbers on the official compare-plans page.

## Setup and integration
Install the extension from Chrome, Firefox, or Edge, then pin the K icon. Free features work with no API key. Paid metrics need a free API key plus an annual plan, entered in the extension settings.

Account details are redacted.](/content-visuals/articles/keywords-everywhere-extension-popup.png "Keywords Everywhere extension popup. Credits were at zero on Sep 21, 2026.")

There is no SDK and nothing to add to the app. The adjustment is research habit: use it on the Google searches and competitor pages that already sit in a launch-page workflow. Turn off autocomplete metrics if a normal session burns credits too fast. Keep App Store keyword work in a separate ASO tool.

## Frequently asked questions
### Is Keywords Everywhere an ASO tool?
No. It overlays Google and other web keyword metrics in a desktop browser. It does not replace App Store listing tools. For store-listing keywords, see [AppTweak](/tools/apptweak) and the [ASO tools guide](/guides/best-aso-tools-for-indie-developers).

### Is Keywords Everywhere free for indie developers?
The extension is free to install, and a long list of features works without credits. Search volume, CPC, competition, and trend data require a paid annual plan. The lowest paid plan is Bronze at $84 per year (checked Sep 21, 2026).

### Does it work on iPhone or Safari?
No. It works on Chrome, Firefox, and Edge. There is no Safari version and no iOS or Android app.

### Do I still need AppTweak if I install Keywords Everywhere?
Yes, if ASO is the job. [AppTweak](/tools/apptweak) is for App Store keyword research. Keywords Everywhere is for Google and other web properties. They do not replace each other.
